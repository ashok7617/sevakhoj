**Subject:** Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 — details of homes/institutions receiving grant-in-aid for senior citizens under the Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC) / Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).

Respected Sir/Madam,

Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information relating to the **Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC)**, a component of the **Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY)** (or its current equivalent scheme for the running and maintenance of Senior Citizen Homes / Old Age Homes / Continuous Care Homes):

1. A complete list of **all organisations / implementing agencies currently sanctioned grant-in-aid** under IPSrC/AVYAY for running and maintenance of Senior Citizen Homes, Old Age Homes, and Continuous Care Homes, for the latest financial year for which data is available. For each, please provide:
   a. Name of the organisation and the home/facility;
   b. Complete postal address (including district, State/UT and PIN code);
   c. Contact details on record (telephone number and email);
   d. Type of home (Old Age Home / Continuous Care Home / etc.) and sanctioned capacity (number of beneficiaries);
   e. Financial year of sanction and amount of grant released.

2. The same list **specifically for the State of Uttar Pradesh**.

3. A copy of any **consolidated or State/UT-wise directory, annexure, or database** of such homes maintained by the Department or by the National Institute of Social Defence (NISD).

4. The **reuse / licensing terms**, if any, applicable to the information at points 1–3 (for example, whether it may be published for public information).

**Note:** I am **not** seeking any personal information of beneficiaries — only institutional/facility details.

**Preferred format:** Please provide the information as a **soft copy by email** to the address below, preferably in a machine-readable format (Excel/CSV), or as a PDF where that is not possible.
