# RTI cover email (English + Hindi)

> **Where to use this:**
> - **rtionline.gov.in (central):** you do NOT email — paste the RTI letter into the "Text of
>   Application" box (attach it as a PDF if it exceeds the character limit) and pay ₹10 online.
> - **Direct email to a PIO:** use only if that office explicitly accepts RTI by email. The ₹10 fee
>   still applies — attach proof of payment, or first confirm the office's accepted fee mode.
> - **Posted / in-person application:** use this as a short covering note on top of the letter.

---

## English

**Subject:** RTI application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 — details of senior-citizen / old-age homes

Respected Sir/Madam,

Please find enclosed my application under the Right to Information Act, 2005, seeking information about old-age homes / senior-citizen homes [ *for the central request:* receiving grant-in-aid under IPSrC / AVYAY, including for Uttar Pradesh ] [ *for the UP request:* run or grant-aided by the Government of Uttar Pradesh ].

The prescribed application fee of ₹10 has been paid via [ online payment / Indian Postal Order No. ____ / treasury challan ] (proof enclosed). I request the information as a **soft copy at this email address, preferably in Excel/CSV**.

Kindly acknowledge receipt. I look forward to your response within 30 days as provided under Section 7 of the Act.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Yours faithfully,
[ Full name ]
[ Address ] · [ Phone ] · [ Email ]
[ Date ]

## हिन्दी (उत्तर प्रदेश हेतु)

**विषय:** सूचना का अधिकार अधिनियम, 2005 के अंतर्गत आवेदन — उत्तर प्रदेश के वृद्धाश्रमों / वरिष्ठ नागरिक गृहों संबंधी सूचना हेतु

महोदय/महोदया,

संलग्न प्रपत्र में सूचना का अधिकार अधिनियम, 2005 के अंतर्गत मेरा आवेदन प्रेषित है, जिसमें उत्तर प्रदेश में संचालित वृद्धाश्रमों / वरिष्ठ नागरिक गृहों (शासकीय एवं अनुदान प्राप्त) की सूची संबंधी सूचना माँगी गई है।

निर्धारित **₹10 आवेदन शुल्क** [ ऑनलाइन / भारतीय पोस्टल ऑर्डर सं. ____ / ट्रेजरी चालान ] के माध्यम से जमा किया गया है (प्रमाण संलग्न)। कृपया सूचना **सॉफ्ट कॉपी में, अधिमानतः Excel/CSV प्रारूप में**, इसी ईमेल पते पर उपलब्ध कराएँ।
