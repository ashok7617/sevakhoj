# RTI Application — Uttar Pradesh Social Welfare Department (State)

> **Before sending:** fill every `[ ]` placeholder. **Verify the correct department & PIO** —
> senior-citizen homes in UP are generally under the Social Welfare Department (Samaj Kalyan
> Vibhag); if a different UP department administers them, the PIO must transfer the request
> under Section 6(3) within 5 days. Confirm the office address on the department website or the
> UP RTI portal. UP RTI fee is ₹10 (postal order / treasury challan, or online if the UP RTI
> portal is available for your district).

**Subject:** Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005 — list of old-age homes / senior citizen homes in Uttar Pradesh (Government-run and grant-aided).

Respected Sir/Madam,

Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information:

1. A complete list of **old-age homes / senior citizen homes / vridha ashrams in Uttar Pradesh** that are:
   (a) **run by the State Government**, and
   (b) **run by NGOs / voluntary organisations with State grant-in-aid, recognition, or registration**.
   For each home, please provide: name; complete address (including district and PIN code); contact details on record (telephone and email); type (residential / day-care); sanctioned capacity; and current operational status (functional / non-functional).

2. The **district-wise** number and list of the above homes.

3. A list of organisations in Uttar Pradesh **receiving grant or assistance** under any State scheme for senior citizens, or under the Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC) / Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY) as implemented in the State, with the name, address, and contact details of each.

4. Whether the Department maintains a **digital directory or database** of such homes. If yes, please provide a copy in a **machine-readable format (Excel/CSV)**.

5. The **reuse / licensing terms**, if any, applicable to the information above (for example, whether it may be published for public information).

**Note:** I am **not** seeking any personal information of residents or beneficiaries — only institutional/facility details.

**Preferred format:** Please provide the information as a **soft copy by email** to the address below, preferably in Excel/CSV, or as a PDF where that is not possible.

I am a citizen of India. The application fee of **₹10** is enclosed / paid via [ Indian Postal Order No. ____ / treasury challan / online (UP RTI portal) ]. [If BPL: I belong to a Below Poverty Line household and am exempt from the fee; a copy of my BPL card is enclosed.]

If any part of this request pertains to another public authority, kindly transfer it under Section 6(3) of the Act and inform me. Kindly provide the information within 30 days as required under Section 7.
